During my internship I took part in three events. Each one was a memorable experience that brought new knowledge along with the chance to meet and exchange ideas with AWS engineers and with the other interns in the program.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Quiz Battle - First Cloud AI Journey kick-off session

&emsp;**Date:** June 20, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee, following the competition as a member of the audience

&emsp;**Main content:** A team competition with four to five members per team, based on a question set covering AWS fundamentals and real scenarios drawn from business requirements

---

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Quiz Battle Grand Final

&emsp;**Date:** July 11, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Main content:** The final round, built around three in-depth topics: SLA and system monitoring, securing web applications with the AWS Security Agent, and the structure of the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)
